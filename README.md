### 🔍 Key Business Insights

1. **Ibadan is HQ:** N83M gross, 88 orders beats Lagos N41M/57 orders by 2x
2. **South West Engine:** 209 orders, N176-178M sales = 38% of business
3. **Discount Crisis:** SW N6.3M + SS N6.4M = 70% of N18.4M total discounts
4. **Channel Paradox:** Showroom N148M top revenue but N6.3-6.8M discount leak. Social N25M revenue with N0.9-1.2M discount = 8x more efficient
5. **Operations Leak:** 71% completion = 182 orders = N130M stuck revenue
6. **Customer Health:** Top 10 = 33% = safe, Real Estate Developer best segment

### ✅ Recommendations

1. **Recover N130M:** Fix 29% failed orders - audit delivery & payment flow
2. **Cap Discounts:** Limit SW/SS to 2% - save N12.7M. Cap Contractor/Builder N9.6M & Showroom N6.3M
3. **Scale Profitable:** Push Social Media (N25M net / N0.9M discount) & Referral N133M
4. **Protect:** Top 10 customers N155.41M - retention program
5. **Push:** Ibadan, Port Harcourt, Contractor/Builder segment (N0.17bn)
